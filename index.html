★ Matches your role

Model deployment · Master’s thesis

AutoDeploy

10.4× ParT · 6.6× ParticleNet

A model-to-deployment platform. It inspects a model, picks an optimization path (TensorRT, ONNX, quantization, pruning) and deploys to GPU, FPGA or NPU, keeping reusable knowledge from every run.

Engineering details +

ParT TensorRT FP16: 0.972 ms, approximately 10.4× eager baseline speed. ParticleNet TensorRT FP32: 0.456 ms, approximately 6.6×. NVIDIA V100, batch size 1, configuration-specific. ParticleNet FP16 showed an accuracy trade-off. CPU ParT reference: 15.7 ms.
